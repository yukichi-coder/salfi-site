Fourgive サポート

Fourgive は、置くコマを相手が選ぶ対戦パズルです。縦・横・斜めの一列、または 2×2 の四角に、同じ特徴のコマを 4 つ並べると勝ちです。

support@salfi.org

Fourgive Support

Fourgive is a puzzle duel where your rival chooses the piece you place. Line up four pieces that share a trait in a row, column, diagonal, or 2×2 square to win.

Contact

Send feedback, bug reports, and questions by email. You can also use "Send feedback" in the App's Settings screen.

support@salfi.org

  • For bugs, please include your device model, OS version, the App version (shown in Settings), and what happened.
  • Replies may take a few days.

Restoring Unlock All

After changing devices or reinstalling, open the Unlock All screen in the App and tap "Restore purchase". Sign in with the Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple. For Google Play, see Google Play refund policies.
