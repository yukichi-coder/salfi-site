1. About the App

Settomo (the "App") helps you set up devices that have a web admin screen (routers, NAS, cameras, printers) from a panel beside the screen. AI reads the screen, points to where to press, answers questions, and suggests tasks. You enter and save settings yourself; the App reads the screen again afterwards to check.

2. Beta and pricing

  • The App is in beta and free during the beta. If pricing is introduced, it will be announced on this page and in the App in advance; you will never be charged without your consent.
  • AI use has a daily limit per device and a daily limit for all testers together. Once a limit is reached, AI is unavailable for the rest of the day (it resets at midnight Japan time).
  • Do not share your invite word or connection code. The operator may stop the connection of a device used excessively or improperly.
  • Beta versions may have bugs and may change or stop without notice.

3. Your responsibility

  • Use the App only on devices you are authorized to manage.
  • Entering and saving settings is your decision. AI answers and guidance can be wrong; check the screen before you save.
  • We recommend backing up the device's settings before changing them. The App guides you through the backup and the way back (recovery card).
  • For firmware updates, resets and reboots, the admin password, remote management, port forwarding, and address or operating-mode changes, the App only explains and points; it never plans or performs them.
  • Device logins and certificate decisions are yours.

  • Using the App for purposes that violate laws or public order
